# Survival Skills for Disaster Scenarios

## Shelter Building
### Emergency Shelter Types
- **Debris hut**: frame of branches, covered with leaves/brush
- **Tarp shelter**: rope between trees, tarp draped over
- **Snow cave**: dig into snowdrift for insulation
- **Vehicle shelter**: stay in vehicle if no safe structure

### Location Requirements
- Away from hazards (flood zones, dead trees, cliffs)
- Near water source (but above flood line)
- Wind protection (behind natural barrier)
- Visible for rescue (open area nearby)

## Water Procurement
### Finding Water
- Rainwater collection (cleanest source)
- Rivers/streams (assume contaminated, always purify)
- Morning dew on vegetation (collect with cloth)
- Melt ice/snow (NEVER eat snow directly - lowers body temp)
- Solar still: dig hole, place container, cover with plastic

### Water Purification Ranking
1. Boiling (MOST reliable) - 1 minute rolling boil
2. Chemical treatment - chlorine/iodine tablets
3. Filtration - commercial filter or layered cloth/charcoal/sand
4. UV treatment - direct sunlight in clear bottle, 6+ hours

### Daily Water Needs
- Drinking: 3-4 quarts per person per day
- Hygiene: 1-2 gallons per person per day
- In hot climate: double the amount

## Fire Building
### Methods
- **Firesteel** (most reliable in wet conditions)
- **Magnifying lens** (requires direct sun)
- **Bow drill** (friction method, practice required)
- **Battery + steel wool** (if available)

### Fire Structure Types
- **Teepee**: kindling in cone shape - quick, hot
- **Log cabin**: stacked log square - long-burning
- **Star**: logs arranged in star, push inward as burning

### Fire Safety
- Clear 10ft area of dry vegetation
- Never leave unattended
- Keep water/dirt nearby for extinguishing
- Ensure complete extinguishment before leaving

## Signaling for Rescue
### Visual Signals
- **Universal SOS pattern**: 3 short, 3 long, 3 short
- Fire: 3 fires in triangle pattern
- Ground markers: large contrasting symbols (X or SOS)
- Flashlight: mirror/screen reflection
- Bright clothing: spread on open ground

### Audible Signals
- Whistle: 3 blasts (universal distress)
- Shouting: 3 shouts
- Tapping on debris: 3 taps
- Horn/vehicle: 3 honks

### When Rescuers Are Near
- Do NOT shout/intermittently signal - conserve energy
- Use bright colors if available
- Stay in open visible area
- Signal during search sweeps

## Navigation Without GPS
- **Sun**: rises East, sets West
- **Stars**: North Star (Polaris) in Northern Hemisphere
- **Shadow stick**: stick in ground, mark shadow tip, wait 15min, mark new tip - East-West line
- **Natural indicators**: moss on north side of trees (Northern Hemisphere)
- **Following water**: streams lead to rivers, rivers lead to civilization

## Food Procurement
### Safe Edible Plants (universal edibility test)
- Test small amount on skin first (wait 15min)
- Test on lip (wait 15min)
- Test on tongue (wait 15min)  
- Eat small amount (wait 8 hours)
- If any reaction: NOT safe

### Universal Signs of Poisonous Plants
- Milky sap
- Three-leaf pattern (poison ivy/oak)
- Umbrella-shaped flower clusters
- Beans/pods in tropical regions (generally unsafe)
- Brightly colored berries (50% poisonous)

### Trapping
- Snares for small game (rabbit trails)
- Deadfall traps (rock balanced on stick)
- Never waste energy chasing game
