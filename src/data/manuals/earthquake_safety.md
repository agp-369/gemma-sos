# Earthquake Safety & Response

## Before Earthquake (Preparedness)
- Secure heavy furniture to walls with brackets
- Store emergency supplies: water (1 gal/person/day), food (3+ days), flashlight, batteries, first aid kit
- Identify safe spots: under sturdy tables, against interior walls
- Practice Drop, Cover, and Hold On drills
- Know how to shut off gas, water, and electricity
- Establish family communication plan

## During Earthquake
### If Indoors:
- DROP, COVER, and HOLD ON
- Stay indoors until shaking stops
- Stay away from windows, glass, light fixtures
- Do NOT use elevators
- If in bed: stay there, cover head with pillow

### If Outdoors:
- Move away from buildings, streetlights, utility wires
- Find an open area away from hazards
- Drop to the ground until shaking stops

### If Driving:
- Pull over to a clear area, stop
- Stay inside the vehicle
- Avoid overpasses, bridges, buildings
- Resume driving cautiously after shaking stops

## After Earthquake
- Check for injuries; administer first aid
- Check for hazards: gas leaks, electrical damage, water pipes
- Evacuate if structure appears damaged
- Be prepared for aftershocks
- Use flashlights (NOT matches/candles) if gas leak suspected
- Listen to battery-powered radio for emergency info
- Stay out of damaged buildings
- Check food/water supplies

## Structural Assessment
- Cracked walls, especially load-bearing walls = danger
- Leaning or shifted building = unsafe to enter
- Fallen ceilings, debris blocking exits
- Listen for creaking, groaning = imminent collapse risk
- Check for: gas smell, exposed wires, water leaks

## Injury Patterns
- Crush injuries from falling debris
- Cuts from shattered glass
- Fractures from being thrown
- Head trauma from falling objects
- Respiratory issues from dust inhalation (wear mask or cloth)
