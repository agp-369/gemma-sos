# Flood Safety & Response

## Before Flood (Preparedness)
- Know flood risk for your area
- Elevate electrical panels, appliances above potential flood levels
- Prepare sandbags if flood-prone
- Store emergency supplies on upper floors
- Document belongings for insurance
- Plan evacuation routes to high ground

## During Flood
### Evacuation
- Move to higher ground immediately
- Do NOT walk through moving water (6" can knock you over)
- Do NOT drive through flooded roads (12" can move a car)
- If trapped: go to highest level, signal for help

### If Driving:
- Turn around, don't drown
- Abandon vehicle immediately if water rises
- Move to roof if car is swept away

### Health Risks
- Do NOT drink floodwater (contaminated with sewage/chemicals)
- Avoid contact with floodwater if you have open wounds
- Watch for snakes, insects in water
- Carbon monoxide risk if using generators indoors

## After Flood
- Do NOT return until authorities say it's safe
- Check for structural damage before entering
- Turn off electricity if standing water present
- Pump out water gradually (fast removal can collapse walls)
- Document damage with photos
- Discard food that contacted floodwater
- Boil water until water supply deemed safe
- Clean and disinfect everything that got wet
- Watch for mold growth within 24-48 hours

## Water Purification Methods
1. Boil: rolling boil for 1 minute (3 min at high altitude)
2. Bleach: 8 drops (1/8 tsp) unscented bleach per gallon, wait 30 min
3. Purification tablets: follow package directions
4. Filter: cloth/coffee filter for sediment, then treat chemically

## Common Flood Injuries
- Hypothermia from cold water exposure
- Wound infections from contaminated water
- Electrocution from downed power lines
- Carbon monoxide poisoning from generators/grills indoors
- Drowning (leading cause of flood deaths)
