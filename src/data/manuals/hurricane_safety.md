# Hurricane & Severe Storm Safety

## Before Hurricane (Preparedness)
- Know evacuation zone and routes
- Board up windows with plywood or storm shutters
- Trim trees and secure loose outdoor items
- Fill vehicle gas tank
- Charge all devices, backup batteries
- Fill bathtubs/containers with water for sanitation
- Prepare: 7+ days food, water, medications, cash, important docs
- Check emergency radio batteries

## Hurricane Warning Categories
- Category 1 (74-95 mph): minimal damage
- Category 2 (96-110 mph): moderate damage
- Category 3 (111-129 mph): devastating damage
- Category 4 (130-156 mph): catastrophic damage
- Category 5 (157+ mph): catastrophic damage, area uninhabitable

## During Hurricane
### If staying home:
- Stay in interior room, away from windows/glass
- Smallest room on lowest floor (NOT basement if flooding risk)
- Interior closet or bathroom
- Lie on floor under sturdy object
- Do NOT go outside during eye of storm

### If evacuating:
- Leave early - do NOT wait for mandatory orders
- Follow official evacuation routes
- Take emergency kit, important documents
- Unplug appliances, turn off gas/electricity
- Lock doors and windows

## After Hurricane
- Stay inside until official all-clear
- Watch for: downed power lines, flooded roads, debris
- Do NOT drive through standing water
- Check for gas leaks - if smell gas, evacuate immediately
- Use flashlights, NOT candles if gas suspected
- Beware of: snakes, insects driven to higher ground
- Inspect home for structural damage with flashlight
- Document damage before cleanup
- Check on neighbors, especially elderly

## Common Hurricane Injuries
- Lacerations from flying debris
- Head trauma
- Drowning from storm surge/flooding
- Electrocution from downed lines
- Carbon monoxide poisoning (generators must be OUTSIDE, 20ft from home)
- Hypothermia from exposure

## Tornado Safety (Hurricane-spawned)
- Go to basement, storm cellar, or interior room on lowest floor
- Stay away from windows, doors, outside walls
- Lie flat in ditch/culvert if outside and no shelter
- Cover head and neck
- Mobile homes are UNSAFE - evacuate to permanent structure
