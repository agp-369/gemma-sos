# Gemma-SOS: Video Pitch Script
**Duration**: 2:45 (under 3 min limit)
**Target Track**: Global Resilience ($10k) + Main Track ($50k)
**Special Tracks**: LiteRT + Ollama + Unsloth
**Core Focus**: Medical Triage — all other features are secondary

---

## STRUCTURE

| Time | Section | Visual |
|------|---------|--------|
| 0:00 | Hook | Dark screen → flickering phone light |
| 0:15 | The Problem | News footage of disaster aftermath |
| 0:45 | The Solution | App launch → triage dashboard |
| 1:15 | Feature Demo: START Triage (MAIN EVENT) | Full triage workflow — NL input, RED classification, patient list, confidence badges |
| 2:15 | Technical Depth + Bonus Tools | Model graphic + quick glance at wreckage/maps/SOS |

## SCRIPT

### 0:00 - HOOK (15s)
**Visual**: Complete darkness. A phone screen flickers on. Text message appears character by character.
**Audio**: Subtle static, then a heartbeat.

**Narrator**: "When the earthquake hits. When the flood waters rise. When the grid goes dark... your phone is still in your hand. But without the internet, it's just a brick."

### 0:15 - THE PROBLEM (30s)
**Visual**: Split screen - news footage of disaster scenes (earthquake rubble, flood waters, hurricane damage) vs. a phone showing "No Service"
**Audio**: News audio fades into somber background music

**Narrator**: "The first 72 hours after a disaster are critical. That's when most lives are saved or lost. But it's also when internet connectivity vanishes, cloud AI becomes useless, and people are left alone to make life-or-death decisions with no guidance."

**Narrator**: "Medical triage, structural assessment, finding shelter - these tasks require expertise that most people don't have. And the experts can't reach everyone in time."

### 0:45 - THE SOLUTION (30s)
**Visual**: App icon appears → splash screen → "AI Guardian Active" status card
**Audio**: Music shifts to hopeful, building

**Narrator**: "This is Gemma-SOS. An offline AI disaster response system that runs entirely on your phone. Powered by Google's Gemma 4 E2B - a frontier AI model that fits in under 3 GB and runs completely offline."

**Narrator**: "No internet. No cloud. No data leaving your device. Just you, your phone, and the intelligence of Gemma 4, working together."

### 1:15 - FEATURE DEMO: START TRIAGE (60s — MAIN EVENT)
**Visual**: Screen recording — tap hero "START TRIAGE NOW" button → NL input "unconscious, not walking, breathing slowly" → RED classification appears with confidence badge → patient list fills with color-coded entries
**Audio**: Keyboard clicks, haptic feedback sounds

**Narrator**: "This is the heart of Gemma-SOS. A patient is found in the rubble. Anyone — a neighbor, a volunteer, a family member — can perform professional-grade triage. Describe what you see in plain language, or use the manual form for precise measurements."

**On-screen**: "RED / IMMEDIATE — Confidence: HIGH — RR: 8/min"

**Narrator**: "Behind the scenes, the START protocol decision tree runs deterministically — no hallucinations, no guesswork. Combined with Gemma 4's natural language understanding, this system can triage hundreds of casualties in minutes."

**Visual**: Switch to manual form, demonstrate RR slider, pulse toggle, cap refill. Show new patient added to list.
**Audio**: UI interaction sounds

**Narrator**: "RED means immediate evacuation. YELLOW can wait minutes. GREEN can help others. BLACK is beyond saving. Every patient is logged with timestamp, GPS position, and a confidence score — so responders know exactly how reliable each assessment is."

**Visual**: Show the growing patient list with color-coded entries, confidence badges visible
**Audio**: Soft notification as each patient is added

**Narrator**: "This turns one phone into a field hospital command center. No internet. No cloud. Just Gemma 4 E2B, running on-device, saving lives."

### 2:15 - TECHNICAL DEPTH + BONUS TOOLS (20s)
**Visual**: Graphic showing Gemma 4 on-device architecture, LiteRT-LM logo. Quick swipe through wreckage analysis, maps, SOS screens
**Audio**: Tech-ambient background

**Narrator**: "Under the hood: Gemma 4 E2B on LiteRT-LM — 2.58 GB, 128K context, multi-token prediction for 2x faster decode. Fine-tuned with Unsloth LoRA on disaster-specific data. Plus bonus tools for wreckage analysis, offline maps, and emergency SOS — all running completely offline."

### 2:35 - CLOSE (10s)
**Visual**: Phone in hand, person walking toward sunrise over disaster zone. Text appears: "Gemma-SOS: When the grid goes down, intelligence doesn't have to."
**Audio**: Music swells, then fades

**Narrator**: "When the grid goes down... intelligence doesn't have to. Gemma 4, running on your phone, saving lives. That's AI for good."

**On-screen**: "Kaggle Gemma 4 Good Hackathon | Global Resilience Track"

## PRODUCTION NOTES

### Required Materials
- [ ] Flutter app build recording (Android emulator or physical device)
- [ ] Screen recording with clean UI interactions
- [ ] Disaster footage (use Creative Commons / royalty-free)
- [ ] Professional voiceover or clean narration
- [ ] Background music (royalty-free, somber→hopeful arc)

### Technical Requirements
- Resolution: 1920x1080 minimum
- Captions: Enable YouTube auto-captions + upload custom .srt
- Format: MP4, H.264
- YouTube link must be unlisted (public for judging)

### Scoring Checklist
- [ ] Clear problem statement (Hook + Problem: 45s)
- [ ] Visible working demo — START triage is **60% of demo time** (Features: 1min)
- [ ] Bonus tools shown briefly (wreckage, maps, SOS: 20s)
- [ ] Technical depth explanation (Tech: 20s)
- [ ] Human impact / emotional story (Close: 10s)
- [ ] "Wow" factor demonstrated — AI triage on a phone, offline
- [ ] Professional production quality

### Keywords for Description
Gemma 4, Gemma 4 Good Hackathon, offline AI, disaster response, LiteRT, MediaPipe, Flutter, on-device AI, medical triage, multimodal AI, Unsloth, Ollama, edge AI, Kaggle
